How predictive coding explains sensory gain control

Placeholder Author et al. · 2024

Reframes attention as a precision-weighting problem rather than a resource one. The core claim is that gain isn't allocated by a separate spotlight mechanism but falls directly out of how confident the model is in its own predictions at each level of the hierarchy. That reframing is why it's on the desk right now — it changes how I'm thinking about the current model, and probably means a chunk of the discussion section needs to be rewritten.

Denoising calcium traces without losing fast transients

Placeholder Author · 2023

A cleaner alternative to the pipeline I've been running, using a learned prior instead of a fixed exponential kernel. The tradeoff is a heavier compute cost per session, which matters at the scale I'm processing recordings at, but the fast-transient preservation looks noticeably better on the noisy sessions. Worth a re-implementation pass this month before the next batch of imaging comes in.
